Customer Acquisition Value Intelligence

Stevens Institute of Technology · MIS 636 · Team 3 · Synthetic digital bank, July 1, 2026 snapshot

How should a digital bank decide where to invest its customer acquisition budget? Revenue ROAS tells part of the story. This prototype adds what customers contribute after funding, servicing, rewards, and credit losses, and compares groups only at the same customer age.

How it works

  1. Connect acquisition to customers. Campaign spend, marketing touches, registrations, and product accounts are linked. Attribution uses the last eligible touch within 30 days before signup; unresolved attribution stays visible as unknown.
  2. Measure realized contribution. Activity across savings, debit, credit cards, personal loans, and mortgages becomes revenue and cost entries. Deposits and loan principal stay outside earnings.
  3. Freeze what was expected at signup. A rule-based first-year contribution estimate, with its expected path at each checkpoint, is recorded at acquisition and never revised.
  4. Compare at the same age. A cohort that has not reached a checkpoint shows as pending, not as a low number.

Cohort summary

Cumulative contribution per customer

Each line is one signup cohort followed as its customers age. The start cohort is highlighted, with a dashed line for the contribution expected at signup: a fixed rule-based estimate that is never revised, so a large gap is a finding to investigate rather than an error. A line stops at the last age every customer in that cohort has reached. Contribution can fall between ages when a period's costs, such as credit losses or rewards, exceed its revenue. Hover over a line or legend entry to isolate it.

Contribution per customer by channel

One line per acquisition channel across the selected cohorts, measured at the same customer age.

Each point is the average realized contribution per customer for that channel and cohort. A gap means that cohort has not reached the selected age yet. The dashed line is the average across all signups. Not affected by the channel filter above.

Cohort table

Realized contribution per customer at each age, with the signup-time expectation underneath.

Darker cells mean higher contribution; red cells are negative. Pending cells contain customers who have not reached that age yet.

Signups by acquisition channel

Where each cohort's customers came from. A shift in channel mix can explain why cohorts perform differently.

Counts include every signup, regardless of the channel filter above.

Channel efficiency by cohort

One line per paid channel across the selected cohorts.

Spend is counted in the month it was paid. ROAS always uses revenue at 90 days; the other metrics use the customer age selected above. Signups with an unknown channel have no spend and are left out. Not affected by the channel filter.

# Workstream B · Data contract

v1.0 · Frozen physical contract. Economic authority: [Frozen project specification](01_project_spec.md). The source-only fixture and case inventory are under [data/validation_cases](../data/validation_cases/README.md). See the [logical ERD](../exhibits/architecture/logical_erd.md).

## Approved decisions and physical conventions

Keep the project scope and economics in A, including the three paid channels, unknown attribution, five products, all eligible spend, fixed acquisition forecasts and age-matched contribution. The synthetic ledger uses confirmed principal charge-offs and subsequent recoveries. Provision-based reporting is outside this prototype. The equal-multiplier sensitivity changes only the channel adjustment and preserves baseline forecasts and acquisition spend.

Notation: `T` = VARCHAR, `TS` = TIMESTAMPTZ, `D` = DATE, `C` = BIGINT USD cents, `I` = INTEGER, `B` = BOOLEAN, `R` = DECIMAL(12,8) for source rates, `Q` = DECIMAL(28,8) for reporting values, `J` = JSON. A question mark means nullable. Every other field is required. PK denotes both grain and uniqueness. Arrows denote foreign keys. Boolean and lifecycle codes apply only where shown. Prototype IDs are opaque, synthetic and stable across reruns. A logical namespace prefix distinguishes visitors and source records across systems. Enforce cross-table and temporal checks in ELT where DuckDB constraints cannot express them.

All timestamps are UTC. Source availability uses recorded_at. Economic placement uses effective_at. A batch records an exclusive effective completeness cutoff and inclusive recorded-at cutoff. Historical windows are half-open at the customer-age endpoint. Preserve a stable input manifest and both cutoffs per run. Spend dates and month/period keys are UTC, with month dates set to the first day.

### Product activity constraints

Each source activity row supplies one amount or one daily balance/rate basis. Amount in source is nonnegative; adjustment direction comes from the adjustment kind. Per-code required fields apply in addition to the shared envelope. Set unused fields to null. One balance basis per account × UTC day × balance kind, after source deduplication. Daily accrual uses the agreed A day-count and rounding. Canonical direct component uses the A event code as activity_code. For accrual codes listed below, derive cents from balance and rate. All other direct financial components use amount_cents. SETTLED, POSTED and CONFIRMED qualify where appropriate to the activity. PENDING and VOID do not create economics or activation.

| Product | Source-only and accrual rows | Financial mappings |
|---|---|---|
| SAVINGS | DEP_SETTLED and WITHDRAWAL are non-economic cash movements. In the frozen fixture DEP_SETTLED denotes an external settled deposit and supplies activation evidence under A. Balance kinds SAV_SETTLED_TREASURY, SAV_SETTLED_DEPOSITOR. | SAV_TREASURY_INCOME and SAV_DEPOSIT_INTEREST are daily accruals. SAV_FEE and SAV_SERVICE are direct. |
| DEBIT | PURCHASE_SETTLED and PURCHASE_REFUND represent purchase face values; the refund links to purchase source. | DEBIT_INTERCHANGE, DEBIT_FEE, DEBIT_PROCESSING, DEBIT_REWARD, DEBIT_FRAUD, DEBIT_SERVICE are direct. |
| CREDIT | PURCHASE_SETTLED, PURCHASE_REFUND and PRINCIPAL_REPAYMENT are non-economic. Balance kinds CREDIT_PERFORMING_REVOLVING and CREDIT_FUNDED. | CREDIT_INTEREST and CREDIT_FUNDING accrue; CREDIT_INTERCHANGE, CREDIT_FEE, CREDIT_PROCESSING, CREDIT_REWARD, CREDIT_SERVICE, CREDIT_LOSS are direct. |
| PERSONAL | LOAN_FUNDING, PRINCIPAL_REPAYMENT and INTEREST_RECEIPT are cash movements. Balance kinds PERSONAL_PERFORMING_PRINCIPAL and PERSONAL_FUNDED. | PERSONAL_INTEREST and PERSONAL_FUNDING accrue; PERSONAL_FEE, PERSONAL_SERVICE, PERSONAL_COLLECTION, PERSONAL_LOSS are direct. |
| MORTGAGE | LOAN_FUNDING, PRINCIPAL_REPAYMENT and INTEREST_RECEIPT are cash movements. Balance kinds MORTGAGE_PERFORMING_PRINCIPAL and MORTGAGE_FUNDED. | MORTGAGE_INTEREST and MORTGAGE_FUNDING accrue; MORTGAGE_FEE, MORTGAGE_ORIGINATION, MORTGAGE_SERVICE, MORTGAGE_LOSS are direct. |

Use the account product to reject invalid code combinations. Optional component on direct rows may reference a related operational activity through original_source_key when linkage is needed. For ADJUSTMENT, require amount_cents, original_source_key, original component event code in component and adjustment_kind in RESTATEMENT_REDUCTION/LATER_REDUCTION/RECOVERY. Reductions are signed negative in the original class. Recovery is allowed only against realized loss. For restatements the canonical effective time inherits the original. For later reductions and recoveries it uses the adjustment event time. Reject missing/unavailable original, class mismatch and reductions exceeding the original after other accepted adjustments. Use event effective time, then recorded time, then stable source_key to order competing reductions. Proposed ingestion convention: an explicit duplicate re-delivery shares source availability and has a distinct delivery_id. The source-only operational PURCHASE_REFUND can link to the purchase for net activation independently of interchange/reward corrections.

Channel cohort compares only fully mature months for realized metrics. Retain registration counts/spend and frozen forecasts for pending periods. Materialize unknown cohort rows with null paid metrics. Distinguish confirmed spend-only rows from absent source spend. Spend-only rows retain spend, have zero registrations and store both CAC and ROAS as null with ZERO_DENOMINATOR. Display both as N/A. Calculate ratios from exact integer inputs and round once to eight decimal places, half-up (ties away from zero). Missing or incomplete plans leave cumulative plan and pace_ratio null and pacing_status UNAVAILABLE. Join attribution and activation at their declared grains before financial aggregation. Filter to a single scenario and spend method or group explicitly by both.

## Draft generation plan and decisions

1. Freeze rules, schema and D’s independently calculated fixtures before writing the full generator. Pin a deterministic seed and generator version.
2. Generate approximately 5,000 canonical customers across the 18 months, campaigns, independent signup intent, visitor links and touches with controlled lag. Include enough early 2025 registrations to support mature 365-day comparisons and enough late registrations to exhibit censoring.
3. Generate campaign calendar and spend independently from registration, with planned budgets, sparse days, zero-registration spend and cross-month winning touches. Collect business totals by campaign/day before transformation.
4. Generate product ownership and conditional actions across all five products, with funded balances, settlement, fees and direct costs. Preserve realistic ordering and accounting identities, and permit multi-product holdings. Document distributions and separate observed synthetic activity from forecast assumptions.
5. Introduce tagged duplicate deliveries, late availability, attribution gaps, charge-offs and corrections, and preserve source lineage. Export ordered JSONL or typed CSV plus manifest with seed, file hashes, table counts and cutoff. Freeze source extract for Assignment 3.

Approved: choices in A and the B request, including the realized-loss limitation and separate equal-channel sensitivity. Frozen: physical interface, timestamp rules, correction ordering and published baseline and equal-channel approach. Generation choices still to document: seed, distributions, channel/intent mix and simulated exception frequencies. Use A’s thresholds as prototype defaults.

## Handoffs

**D first:** Read the source rows and independently calculate and sign off expected values listed in the case inventory. Write expectations as a separate artifact with per-case formulas, source keys, snapshot/method/version and tolerances (exact cents for money). Prioritize source→ledger→customer→cohort reconciliation. Keep these expectations independent of pipeline output.

**C after freeze and full source generation:** Use this contract and A to implement source manifest ingestion, point-in-time mapping, signed ledger, forecasts, age histories and marts. Preserve rejected/deduplicated records and source pointers. Emit reconciliations for both spend methods and forecast scenarios at matching populations. Use D’s expectations as comparison inputs only.

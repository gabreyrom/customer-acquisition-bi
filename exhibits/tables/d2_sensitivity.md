# Two assumptions change different quantities

## A. March 2025 mature cohort, 90-day revenue

The customer population and revenue numerator stay fixed. Winning-touch allocation moves eligible spend among signup months, changing CAC and ROAS through the spend denominator or numerator.

| Channel | Signups | Calendar spend | Touch-weighted spend | CAC, calendar → touch | ROAS, calendar → touch |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Google Ads | 86 | $6,218.43 | $5,970.40 | $72.31 → $69.42 | 2.29× → 2.39× |
| Meta Ads | 101 | $7,214.87 | $6,866.49 | $71.43 → $67.99 | 1.46× → 1.53× |
| Paid affiliates | 70 | $5,422.72 | $5,873.77 | $77.47 → $83.91 | 2.21× → 2.04× |

March ordering is unchanged. Across the 15 mature months, CAC ordering changes in six and ROAS ordering in one. February 2025's lowest CAC switches from Meta to affiliates. December 2025 swaps the second and third ROAS positions, with Meta first under both methods. Each method preserves total source spend of $330,676.79 across the full history.

Equal-multiplier differences are attributable to model-input composition in this fixed sample. All figures represent synthetic model behavior. Channel rankings do not establish marketing effectiveness.
